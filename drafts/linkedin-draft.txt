Built Nabdah (نبضة): a free, Arabic-first crowdsourced coverage map for emergencies and hiking.
Report your signal in two taps, see the nearest place with signal when you have none, check a hike route for dead stretches, record trails offline. Reports are snapped to a ~275 m grid, no accounts.
Demo data for now; it gets real as people report.
