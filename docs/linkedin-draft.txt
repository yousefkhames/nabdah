Built Nabdah (نبضة): a free, Arabic-first crowdsourced coverage map for emergencies and hiking.
Report your signal in two taps, see where the nearest signal is when you have none, record trails offline and export GPX. Reports are snapped to a ~275 m grid, no accounts.
Demo data for now; it gets real as people report.
